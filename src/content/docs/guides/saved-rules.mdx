These changes are **unreleased**. They do not verify the live site or native
settlement. This page adds to the preparation guide proposed in
[PR #97](https://github.com/bitcoinuniverseio/docs-stampdex/pull/97), which remains
open.

Prerequisites: connect the wallet that will review the action.

1. In Desk, prepare a buy or mint request. Choose the exact deployment.
2. Open **Watch price or fees**. Set a time, optional limits and expiry.
3. Choose **Save rule** and authorize wallet access.
4. Open **Saved rules** on Desk. Choose **Review** when a draft is ready.

The backend watches while the browser is closed. It prepares a review. You still
confirm the terms and sign with your wallet. A draft does not buy or mint.
Unknown prices, fees or network data do not meet a limit.

Use **Pause**, **Resume** or **Cancel rule** to control a watch. A daily rule
waits for you to dismiss its current review before preparing the next one.
It skips missed runs. It does not queue catch-up buys.

On an owned order, open **Watch this order** to watch progress or listing expiry
for seven days. Expired listings return to a new listing review. The form checks
ownership and available quantity again. A transaction seen by the network does
not prove final delivery.

Mint preparation carries the amount and batch count into the existing mint form.
Saved mint recovery keeps the original transaction identity. Reconcile an unknown
submission before retrying.

Text and local QR capture remain available. AI, voice and document extraction
still need the approved inference service. SRC-20 settlement uses escrow.

## verification

The rule appears under **Saved rules** with its current state. A ready rule
opens a review, not a completed purchase. Use the saved order or mint progress
to check the actual result.
