Everything except your own holdings is public. You need a wallet only to buy, sell, or
see what an address you control holds.

## 1. Open the market

Go to [stampdex.fun](https://stampdex.fun). The landing view is the SRC-20 market
board: every token with its floor, volume, holders, and mint progress, and the
deployment transaction that identifies it. `/src20` and `/markets` open the same board.

Click a token to open its page. See [The market board](/docs-stampdex/guides/browse-the-market/).

## 2. Explore stamps

[stampdex.fun/stamps](https://stampdex.fun/stamps) is the stamps side: the artwork
index, collections, live listings, and search. Every stamp page shows the artwork with
its on-chain identity, including its number, creator, supply, and file hash.

## 3. Check the market pulse

`/desk` is the trading desk: volume boards, mint velocity, floor movers, open mints, and
fee pressure in one place. `/activity` shows recent marketplace events.

## 4. Connect a wallet when you want to act

Buying, selling, and seeing your own holdings need a connected wallet. Universe Wallet,
UniSat, Leather, Xverse, and OKX Wallet are supported, and each can do a different set
of things here. See [Wallets](/docs-stampdex/reference/wallets/).

Connecting shares your address. Nothing else.

## 5. Know these three things before you trade

1. **A ticker is not an identity.** Check the deployment transaction.
   See [Asset identity](/docs-stampdex/concepts/asset-identity/).
2. **The fee is 1.5% per side, with a 500 sat floor per side.** Both sides pay, so a
   trade carries 3% in total. See [Fees](/docs-stampdex/reference/fees/).
3. **An SRC-20 trade puts your BTC in a StampDEX-controlled escrow while it settles.**
   See [Where your funds are](/docs-stampdex/concepts/custody/).
