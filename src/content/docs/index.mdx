<div class="sd-home-lede">

StampDEX is the marketplace and explorer at [stampdex.fun](https://stampdex.fun) for
two Bitcoin protocols: **Bitcoin Stamps**, artwork written into Bitcoin transactions,
and **SRC-20**, the token standard built on the same mechanism. You can browse the
whole venue without a wallet. Connect one only when you want to trade.

This documentation exists for one reason above the others: a trade on Bitcoin takes
time, and during that time your money is somewhere specific. Every page here says
where.

## The one thing to understand first

Trades are executed at StampDEX, not inside other Bitcoin Universe products. The
registry that describes the Universe ecosystem records both Stamps and SRC-20 as
`mode: external-execution`, owned by StampDEX. Other Universe surfaces can show you a
stamp or a token, but the order book, the matching, and the settlement happen here.

While an SRC-20 trade settles, your BTC is not in your wallet and not in the seller's
wallet. It is in escrow addresses created for that one listing, which StampDEX
controls. StampDEX states that before you sign. Everything on this site uses the same
four colours to say where value sits:
