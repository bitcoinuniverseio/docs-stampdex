# Start here

Five minutes from first visit to knowing your way around. No wallet
needed until step 4.

## 1. Open the market

Go to [stampdex.fun](https://stampdex.fun). The home page is the SRC-20
market: every token with its floor, volume, holders, and mint progress.
Click any token to open its page with the deployment identity, price
history, order book, and live listings.

## 2. Explore stamps

Open [stampdex.fun/stamps](https://stampdex.fun/stamps) for Bitcoin Stamps:
the artwork index, collections, live listings, and search. Every stamp page
shows the artwork with its on-chain identity: stamp number, creator,
supply, and file hash.

## 3. Check the market pulse

[stampdex.fun/desk](https://stampdex.fun/desk) is the trading desk: volume
boards, mint velocity, floor movers, open mints, and fee pressure in one
place. [stampdex.fun/activity](https://stampdex.fun/activity) shows recent
marketplace events.

## 4. Connect a wallet when you want to act

Buying, selling, and seeing your own holdings need a connected wallet:
Universe Wallet, UniSat, Xverse, or OKX. See [Wallets](wallets.md).
Connecting shares your address and nothing else.

## 5. Know before you trade

- [Asset identity](asset-identity.md): how to know what you are buying
- [Fees](fees.md): 1.5% service fee per side, plus the network fee
- [Orders and settlement](orders-and-settlement.md): what each order state
  means and where funds sit
