StampDEX documentation

The trading venue for Bitcoin Stamps and SRC-20. This site documents how a trade is built, where your funds sit at every stage, and what the venue can and cannot do.
Start here Where your funds are Open StampDEX

StampDEX is the marketplace and explorer at stampdex.fun for two Bitcoin protocols: Bitcoin Stamps, artwork written into Bitcoin transactions, and SRC-20, the token standard built on the same mechanism. You can browse the whole venue without a wallet. Connect one only when you want to trade.

This documentation exists for one reason above the others: a trade on Bitcoin takes time, and during that time your money is somewhere specific. Every page here says where.

The one thing to understand first

Section titled “The one thing to understand first”

Trades are executed at StampDEX, not inside other Bitcoin Universe products. The registry that describes the Universe ecosystem records both Stamps and SRC-20 as mode: external-execution, owned by StampDEX. Other Universe surfaces can show you a stamp or a token, but the order book, the matching, and the settlement happen here.

While an SRC-20 trade settles, your BTC is not in your wallet and not in the seller’s wallet. It is in escrow addresses created for that one listing, which StampDEX controls. StampDEX states that before you sign. Everything on this site uses the same four colours to say where value sits:

  • Buyer side: held by the buyer, or delivered to the buyer
  • Seller side: held by the seller, or delivered to the seller
  • Held by StampDEX for the length of one trade
  • Settled on Bitcoin, final
